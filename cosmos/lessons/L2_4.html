Quiz

Choose an answer to see whether it is right, and why. Score 70% or more and you know this lesson.

1. The critical density today corresponds roughly to...

ρc ≈ 9 × 10⁻²⁷ kg/m³ for H0 = 70, about 5.5 hydrogen atoms per cubic metre.

2. If the total density parameter Ω is greater than 1, space is...

Ω + Ωk = 1, so Ω > 1 means Ωk < 0 and k > 0: a closed universe.

3. How does the critical density depend on the expansion rate H?

ρc = 3H²/(8πG): a faster expansion needs a higher density to be flat.

4. In H² = H0²[Ωr a⁻⁴ + Ωm a⁻³ + Ωk a⁻² + ΩΛ], which term dominates at very early times (a → 0)?

a⁻⁴ grows fastest as a becomes small, so radiation dominates the early universe.

5. What is the flatness problem?

For Ω to be close to 1 today, it had to equal 1 to about one part in 10⁶⁰ early on. Inflation is one proposed explanation.
